---
name: Face-free visual direction
description: Visual asset constraint for the salon brand experience.
---

The salon pitch experience should use hair, hands, cropped back-view, tools, product, and interior imagery without recognizable faces.

**Why:** The brand brief explicitly prioritizes face-free imagery to avoid likeness and copyright concerns.

**How to apply:** Verify hero and gallery subjects visually before treating the asset set as final; do not rely on filenames or alt text alone.
